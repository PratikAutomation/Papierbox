# Papierbox — Remotion Video Plan

## Video: 15-second TikTok/Instagram Reel

### Specs
- Resolution: 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)
- Duration: 15 seconds (450 frames at 30fps)
- Format: MP4

### Storyboard (frame by frame)

**Scene 1: Hook (0-3s, frames 0-90)**
- Text flies in: "You're overpaying for groceries in Germany"
- Background: White with green accent
- Emoji: 💸
- Animation: Scale up + fade in

**Scene 2: The Problem (3-6s, frames 90-180)**
- 5 store logos appear one by one: Lidl, Aldi, Penny, Kaufland, Netto
- Text: "5 stores. 5 different prices."
- Text: "Who's cheapest THIS WEEK?"
- Animation: Cards slide in from right

**Scene 3: The Solution (6-10s, frames 180-300)**
- Search bar animation: "Butter" being typed
- Results appearing: price cards from different stores
- Kaufland wins at €1.29
- Text: "Found in 5 seconds"
- Animation: Typing effect + cards sliding up

**Scene 4: CTA (10-15s, frames 300-450)**
- Big: "papierbox.eu"
- Subtitle: "Free. No signup. All 5 stores."
- Background: Green (#22c55e)
- Logo animation: scale + pulse
- Text: "Link in bio 👆"

### Brand Guidelines
- Primary: #22c55e (green)
- Text: #1a1c1c (near black)
- Background: #FAFAFA (off-white)
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (900 weight)
- Style: Neo-brutalist (bold borders, shadows)
