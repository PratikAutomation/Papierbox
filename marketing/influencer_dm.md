### YouTube Creators
- Search "grocery shopping germany" → comment + DM small creators
- Search "cost of living germany" → same approach

## DM Rules

1. **Personalize every DM** — mention their specific content
2. **Never copy-paste the same text** — platforms flag this as spam
3. **No pressure** — "if you like it, share. if not, no worries"
4. **Send 5-10 DMs per day** — not 50 (looks spammy)
5. **Follow them first** — DMs from followers are less likely to be filtered
6. **Best time:** Send between 6-8 PM CET (they're checking DMs)
7. **Follow up once** after 3 days if no response — then move on
